## 1. 🚀 Exploratorium — Pier 15

Hands-on science museum with 600+ interactive exhibits. Currently running **"Life in Space"** exhibit (through Sept 13). Kids can play with everything — water tables, mirrors, physics, optics.

- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Tue–Sat)
- **Admission:** Adults $39.95 · Kids (4–17) $29.95
- **Est. total (4A + 2K):** ~$210
- **Address:** Pier 15, Embarcadero at Green St, SF 94111
- **Website:** https://www.exploratorium.edu
- **Why go:** Best hands-on museum in SF. Kids ages 5–10 will love it.

## 2. 🌿 California Academy of Sciences — Golden Gate Park

Four museums in one: aquarium, planetarium, rainforest dome, and zoo. 3,000+ species under one living roof.

- **Hours:** 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM (Mon–Sat)
- **Admission:** Adults $55 · Kids (3–12) $45
- **Est. total (4A + 2K):** ~$310
- **Address:** 55 Music Concourse Dr, SF 94118
- **Website:** https://www.calacademy.org
- **Why go:** Iconic SF experience. The indoor rainforest alone is worth it.

---

## 3. 🏰 Walt Disney Family Museum — Presidio

Deep dive into Walt Disney's life and work — not a theme park, but an actual museum with animatronics, story reels, and rare artifacts.

- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 5:30 PM (Thu–Mon)
- **Admission:** Adults $30 · Youth (6–17) $15 · Kids 5 & under FREE
- **Est. total (4A + 2K):** ~$135 (kids free!)
- **Address:** 1040 Montgomery St, Presidio of SF, SF 94129
- **Website:** https://www.waltdisney.org
- **Why go:** Surprisingly great even for adults who think they know Disney.

---

## 4. 🎨 de Young Museum — Golden Gate Park

Art and culture museum with a 300-foot observation tower with panoramic views. Currently running **"Treasures of the Pharaohs"** exhibit.

- **Hours:** 10:30 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Admission:** **FREE every Saturday** for Bay Area residents (reserve online, bring ID). Non-residents: $25 adults · free under 17
- **Est. total (4A + 2K):** $0–$100 depending on residency
- **Address:** 50 Hagiwara Tea Garden Dr, SF 94118
- **Website:** https://www.famsf.org/deyoung
- **Why go:** Free + amazing views from the tower. Kids can climb the observation deck.

---

## 5. 🐍 Randall Museum — Stow Lake

Small but charming — live animal exhibits (snakes, lizards, insects), hands-on science/nature displays, and right next to Stow Lake where you can paddle boats.

- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Tue–Sat)
- **Admission:** **FREE**
- **Est. total:** $0
- **Address:** 199 Museum Way, SF 94114
- **Website:** https://randallmuseum.org
- **Why go:** Hidden gem. Combine with a paddle boat rental on Stow Lake right outside.
